About us · Humaneness Labs

Different backgrounds.
A shared curiosity about people.

Humaneness Labs brings together Christoph Schuhmann and Robert Kaczmarczyk — a teacher and a physician, both co-founders of LAION, working on the behavior layer for AI.

Christoph Schuhmann

Education · Open AI research

Christoph is a teacher with a background in physics and computer science. As a co-founder of LAION, he has helped bring together an international community around open datasets, models, and collaborative AI research.

His work in education brings a human perspective to the lab’s research: how we learn, sustain attention, and respond to one another — and what those processes can teach us about building AI.

Robert Kaczmarczyk

Medicine · Open AI research

Robert is a physician and AI researcher, and a co-founder of LAION. His experience spans medicine, research, and the practical work of building an open research community.

He brings a clinical perspective to Humaneness Labs: an attention to context, uncertainty, and the subtle signals that shape communication between people.

From open research to human interaction.

Our shared experience at LAION informs how we work: across disciplines, through collaboration, and with a belief that progress comes from making research accessible.

At Humaneness Labs, we are exploring how attention, emotion, perception, and social context can shape AI behavior. We are starting with voice, where a pause, a change in rhythm, or a moment of hesitation can transform an interaction.
